# Pocket Isle: Meta-style checks (QA round 1, Oct 28 – Nov 2)

You already know the game, so this list is about **the things Meta and the competition judges check** that normal
play won't catch. Do these on top of your usual testing. For each item write **Pass / Fail + one line** (what you did,
what happened, a photo or video if you can). Times are rough.

The competition asks for: **fully usable with hands from start to end**, comfortable **seated**, a **fast start**,
**clean pause and resume**, and **satisfying within about 10 minutes**. Meta's store also has technical checks
(performance, app lifecycle, tracking, comfort). These sections follow those.

## 1. Hands only, start to end (most important)
- [ ] Put the controllers away. Play from launch to the first battle, catch, and into Cinderwood **without touching a
      controller**. Note any moment you got stuck or had to guess what to do.
- [ ] Every button and menu can be used with hands (title runes, game menu, settings, battle runes, lodge).
- [ ] The game menu opens with the left palm-up pinch, every time.

## 2. Seated play and comfort
- [ ] Play 15 minutes **sitting down**. Could you reach everything you needed? Note anything you had to stand or lean far for.
- [ ] Nothing makes you uncomfortable: no sudden camera moves, flashing, or the world moving on its own.
- [ ] Text is readable without leaning in (the Spirit's lines, cards, settings).
- [ ] Recenter island (Settings) puts the island comfortably in front of you.

## 3. Start, pause, resume, exit (app lifecycle)
- [ ] **Cold start:** from tapping the app icon to seeing the game: roughly how many seconds? (fewer than ~10 is good)
- [ ] Press the **Meta / Oculus button** mid-walk and mid-battle: the game pauses; come back: it continues correctly,
      sound comes back, nothing is stuck.
- [ ] **Take the headset off** for 1 minute, put it back on: same as above.
- [ ] **Quit and relaunch** in the middle of a realm: you continue where you were (Continue on the title).
- [ ] Leave the headset idle until it sleeps, wake it: the game is fine.
- [ ] Open the Quest's own menu / notifications while playing: no freeze, no stuck sound.

## 4. Hand tracking edge cases
- [ ] Hide one or both hands (behind your back) for 10 s, bring them back: nothing breaks, nothing triggers by itself.
- [ ] Play in **dim light** and in **bright light**: do grabs and pinches still work? Note where tracking got worse.
- [ ] Do grabs, pokes and pinches work with **either hand**? Try the dominant hand setting (Settings > Dominant hand).

## 5. Performance (watch and feel, no tools needed)
- [ ] Any stutter, freeze or hitch? Note **where** (opening, a realm, a battle, the restoration wave, the finale).
- [ ] Play 30 minutes straight: does the headset get very hot, or does the game slow down over time?
- [ ] Battery: note the % at start and after 30 minutes.

## 6. Mixed reality and space
- [ ] The game starts **without asking you to scan your room**.
- [ ] Try a small space and a big room: the island always appears in a sensible spot in front of you.
- [ ] Walk to the edge of your boundary: the boundary shows and the game doesn't break.

## 7. Crashes and stuck states (anytime)
- [ ] Any crash, black screen or hang: note what you were doing right before. If it happens, try to repeat it once.
- [ ] Any place where you couldn't continue (no way forward, can't leave a room, a task that won't finish).

## 8. The first 10 minutes (what judges see)
After you finish, answer in 3 short lines:
1. What was the **best** moment in the first 10 minutes?
2. Where were you **confused** or bored?
3. Would someone who has never played a game understand what to do?

## How to send
Same as before: one message per issue with Pass/Fail, the section number, what happened, a photo or video.
