I'm making a cozy mixed-reality creature-collecting game for Meta Quest (solo entry, Meta VR Start competition). Help me with concept art and naming. Context first, then the tasks.

GAME
- A floating island hovers in the player's real room. The player is a giant guardian spirit guiding a tiny explorer across it; the island is a window onto a bigger world that scrolls as the explorer walks.
- Story: the world shattered into floating pieces. In each elemental realm the element stopped flowing because a frightened Keeper creature hoards it in a temple. Beat the Keeper and the realm revives. Shards go into a mysterious statue in a neutral hub; when all 3 are placed, the Heart of the World wakes for the finale.
- Guide: a tiny Lantern Spirit that floats by the player's hand. Secret: it is a fragment of the Heart.
- Tone: cozy, whimsical, Ghibli-like warmth. Creatures faint, never get hurt.

ART RULES (important, the 3D is generated from code)
- Toon shaded, flat colour bands, dark outlines. NO textures, no fur strands, no fine detail.
- Creatures are built from smooth, blobby, melted-together rounded shapes (spheres, capsules, rounded cones). Think soft vinyl toys.
- Ears, tails, wings, horns, fins are separate rigid pieces (they rotate on a pivot; no bendy limbs). Short stubby legs.
- Patterns are simple painted zones: belly patch, coloured tips, socks, a few spots or stripes.
- Big readable silhouette, big expressive eyes.
- 100% ORIGINAL. Must not resemble any Pokémon, Digimon, Palworld or other existing creature. Avoid electric rodents, red/white balls, and any famous starter look.

REALMS
1. Still Isle (neutral hub): calm grey-green meadow, mossy stone ruins, a big weathered statue with 3 empty sockets, a Hearth Shrine (small shrine with a warm fire).
2. Fire realm: ember autumn forest. Red-orange trees with glowing leaves, hot springs, pottery kilns. Temple: a forge cut into a cliff. Before restoring: leaves grey, kilns cold.
3. Water realm: lily lagoon. Lily pads, stilt huts, waterfalls, shallow coral pools. Temple half-sunk in a lake. Before: waterfalls frozen mid-fall, lagoon drying.
4. Wind realm: windmill highlands. Rolling hills, windmills, kites, tall waving grass. Temple: a tall wind tower. Before: still windmills, kites on the ground.

TASKS (reply in this order, with images where asked)
A. For each realm: 1 top-down 3/4 diorama concept image (small floating chunk of land, toon style, rocky underside) in its RESTORED state, plus a 6-colour hex palette and a list of 8–10 props/tiles.
B. Keepers, one image each (front + side view on plain background), plus 2 lines on personality and why it's scared:
   - Hearthstag: great stag with ember antlers (Fire)
   - Tidemother: giant turtle with a lily garden on her shell (Water)
   - Galeowl: huge owl tangled in kite strings (Wind)
C. Lantern Spirit: front + side view. Tiny, floating, warm glow, readable at palm size.
D. Heart of the World: one image. Huge, ancient, combines hints of all three elements and the hub's mossy stone.
E. 3 starter creatures (one Fire, one Water, one Wind), front + side view each, following the art rules. Names for each.
F. 10 name ideas for the game (short, original, easy to say, check they're not existing games), and better names for the 4 realms.
G. Music direction: one paragraph per realm + hub (instruments, tempo, mood), written as a Lyria prompt.

Keep text short and scannable. I'll bring your results back to my other tools.
