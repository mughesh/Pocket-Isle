only add files under `Assets/Docs/ConceptArt/`, don't commit). Match the style of `05_fire_first4.png`.

Redraw these three, front + side view each, one PNG: `10_fire_redraws.png`.
1. **Embercrane** (rare surprise, should feel special): an elegant, proud crane, not a plain grey bird.
   Thick rounded neck, cream and charcoal body, a crest and fan tail of chunky glowing ember feathers,
   red cap on the head. Calm, wise expression.
2. **Cindercap**: the current one looks too much like a classic video-game mushroom person (spotted cap on a
   pale body). Make it a lantern-like mushroom: dark charcoal cap with glowing ember cracks instead of spots,
   a short warm-orange stem body, and tiny ember spores drifting up.
3. **Steamswan** (evolution of Kettlewing, see 05/06 sheets): keep the kettle family look. The body is a round
   ceramic kettle shape, the lid is a little hat, the beak is the spout, steam puffs from it, the wings are
   chunky handles. A swan silhouette, but clearly a kettle.

Append the three to `Concepts_02_Summary.md` in the same block format (body, rigid parts, pattern zones,
palette, idle motion).
