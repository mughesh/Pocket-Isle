# Build plan

Goal before Goa (Oct 28): a playable vertical slice on Quest.
The island sits on your real table, the explorer walks with thumb gestures, tall grass triggers
Emberpup, and a flick throws the capture pod onto the shelf. Battle comes after Goa.

Each step ends with something visible. Commit after each step.

| Step | Target date | What you see at the end |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Toon look in Unity | Oct 21–22 | Island, wavy grass, trees and rocks in a Diorama scene, toon shaded, in the Editor |
| 2. On the headset | Oct 21–22 | APK on Quest: passthrough, island anchored on your real table via MRUK (fallback: in front of you), thumb microgesture test |
| 3. Creature baker | Oct 22–24 | Editor tool sculpts Emberpup into a mesh asset; it idles on the island (blink, tail wag, flame) |
| 4. Walk and encounter | Oct 24–25 | Explorer walks tile to tile with microgestures, tall grass rustles, "!" pops, Emberpup jumps out |
| 5. Capture | Oct 26–27 | Flick to throw the pod, wobble, catch, creature appears on the shelf. Tagged build before Goa |
| Goa | Oct 28 – Nov 2 | No work |
| 6. Battle | Nov 3–6 | Partner vs wild, gaze + pinch move runes, 4 attack templates with effects |
| 7. More creatures | Nov 6–9 | Jellop, Breezle + 3 new recipes from Gemini concept sheets |
| 8. Polish | Nov 9–13 | Onboarding (first 5 min), sound, collection progress, softened island art, performance pass to 72 fps |
| 9. Ship | Nov 14–16 | Demo video, Devpost write-up, APK to the Competition release channel |

Cut list if late: extra creatures beyond 3, scrolling map, battle shields, sound polish.
